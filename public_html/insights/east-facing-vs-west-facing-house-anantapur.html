East-Facing vs West-Facing House: A Vastu Guide for Anantapur Home Buyers

House facing is one of the first questions Anantapur families ask. Here is a simple guide to east-facing and west-facing homes — Vastu, sunlight, heat and layout.

Vastu & design · 5 min read · 24 Oct 2026

“Is it east-facing?” is often the very first question a family in Anantapur asks about a new home. Facing matters for Vastu, but also for practical reasons like sunlight and heat. Here is a balanced guide to help you choose.

What does “facing” mean?

A house's facing is the direction you look towards when you stand at the main entrance and look out to the road. So an east-facing house has its main entrance on the east side, facing the road to the east.

East-facing homes

In Vastu Shastra, east is associated with the rising sun, and east-facing homes are traditionally seen as auspicious. Practical benefits:

  • Morning sunlight enters the front of the house, which is pleasant and keeps the entrance bright.
  • Cooler evenings at the front, because the harsh afternoon sun falls on the back of the house.
  • Strong resale demand, since many buyers specifically look for east-facing homes.

West-facing homes

West-facing homes are often misunderstood. Vastu experts generally agree that a west-facing house with a well-planned layout is perfectly good, and it can suit people in certain professions. Practical points:

  • Evening light brightens the living areas at the front.
  • Afternoon heat falls on the front of the house — in Anantapur's summers, good shading, balconies and planting help a lot.
  • Often better value, because demand for east-facing homes is higher.

Layout matters more than facing

A thoughtful layout can make either facing work well. When you look at a floor plan, check:

  1. Where the kitchen is placed (south-east is traditionally preferred in Vastu)
  2. Where the master bedroom is placed (south-west is traditionally preferred)
  3. Whether every room has a window for cross-ventilation
  4. How the staircase and toilets are positioned

Frequently asked questions

Is an east-facing house better than a west-facing house?

East-facing homes are traditionally preferred in Vastu and get morning sun at the front. A west-facing home with a good layout is also considered good, and is often better value.

Does MSS Balaji Grandeur have east-facing homes?

Yes. Both 2 BHK and duplex homes at MSS Balaji Grandeur are available in east-facing and west-facing options, each with its own floor plan.

How do I reduce heat in a west-facing house in Anantapur?

Use shaded balconies, light-coloured exterior paint, UPVC windows with tinted glass, trees or planters at the front and good cross-ventilation.
