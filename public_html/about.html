About us

MSS Builders — Mark of Quality. We build homes where families can live well today and see real value for years to come.

Our story

MSS Builders has been part of Anantapur's real estate journey for 15 years. Our aim has always been simple: to offer good property where every buyer benefits — in the short term and the long term.

We believe in giving the best quality and assurance of living space, so that the dreams of every family and investor are realised. From our first villas on Kalyandurgam Road to the gated communities at Aravakuru, every project has been built with that promise in mind.

Today, families live in four completed MSS communities across Anantapur — and our latest project, MSS Balaji Grandeur, brings 2 BHK and duplex homes to a new gated community.

Leadership

A message from our Managing Director

When we started MSS Builders 15 years ago, I made a simple promise — every family who buys an MSS home should feel they made the right decision, on the day they move in and for many years after.

That promise has guided us from Balaji Villas on Kalyandurgam Road and SK University Road to MSS Balaji Grandeur 1 and 2 at Aravakuru. Today, families live happily in all four of our completed communities, and their trust means more to us than anything else.

With MSS Balaji Grandeur on Kalyandurgam Road, near Intell College, we are bringing that same care to a new gated community — strong construction, wide roads, underground electricity and drainage, a clubhouse and round-the-clock security, at honest and negotiable prices.

I personally invite you to visit the site, walk through the homes and meet our team. We would be happy to help you find the right home for your family.

— Dwaram Siva Bhaskar ReddyManaging Director, MSS Builders · Mark of Quality
