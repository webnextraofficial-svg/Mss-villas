FAQs

What is MSS Grandeur?

MSS Grandeur is a residential project by MSS Builders offering 2 BHK and duplex homes in a gated community in Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh.

Where is MSS Grandeur located?

MSS Grandeur is in Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh. Call 92979 75656 for directions or to schedule a site visit.

What home options are available?

You can choose between 2 BHK homes and duplex homes, both within the same gated community.

What is the price of a 2 BHK home?

2 BHK homes are priced at ₹64 Lakhs. Prices are negotiable — call us to discuss.

What is the price of a duplex home?

Duplex homes are priced at ₹81 Lakhs. Prices are negotiable — call us to discuss.

Are the prices negotiable?

Yes. Prices for both 2 BHK and duplex homes are negotiable. Call 92979 75656 to talk to our team.

What community features does MSS Grandeur offer?

MSS Grandeur has 40 FT CC roads, a gated community layout, a clubhouse and 24/7 security.

Is MSS Grandeur a gated community?

Yes. MSS Grandeur is a gated community with round-the-clock security.

How do I book a site visit?

Fill in the booking form on this page or call 92979 75656. Our team will call you back to fix a convenient time.

How can I get full project details?

Send an enquiry using any form on this website or call us — our team will share the latest details on availability, plans and pricing.

Who is the builder of MSS Grandeur?

MSS Grandeur is developed by MSS Builders — Mark of Quality — a company that has been building homes in Anantapur for 15 years.

Which projects has MSS Builders completed?

MSS Builders has completed Balaji Villas Phase 1 (Kalyandurgam Road), Balaji Villas Phase 2 (SK University Road), MSS Balaji Grandeur 1 and MSS Balaji Grandeur 2 (both at Aravakuru) in Anantapur.
