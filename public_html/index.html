FAQ's

What services does Ghar Upgrade provide?

Interior design, painting, waterproofing and AC works for residential and commercial properties in Hyderabad.

Can I get interiors, painting, waterproofing and AC work from one team?

Yes. We can plan and coordinate all four services together, so you deal with one company instead of managing separate contractors.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Hyderabad and nearby areas within about 18 km. If you are just outside this area, contact us and we will confirm whether we can take up your project.

How can I contact customer service?

Call +91 63026 60352 or chat on WhatsApp for a quick response.

How do I book a site visit?

Fill in the booking form on this page, call +91 63026 60352, or message us on WhatsApp. Our team will call you back to fix a convenient time.

Is the site visit free?

Yes. You can book a free site visit — our expert checks your space and understands your requirements before suggesting a solution.

Will I get a quotation before work starts?

Yes. After the site visit we share a clear, itemised quotation covering the scope of work, materials and charges, so there are no surprises later.

Do you provide a warranty?

Warranty terms depend on the service, products used and scope of work. Any applicable warranty is clearly mentioned in your quotation.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the service, the size of the space and the work involved. We share an estimated timeline with your quotation, before work begins.

Do you work on offices and commercial spaces?

Yes. Along with homes, apartments and villas, we take up projects for offices, shops and other commercial properties.

Will my furniture and floors be protected during work?

Yes. Our team covers furniture, floors and fixtures before starting, and cleans up the work area once the job is done.

Can you fix leakage and dampness before painting?

Yes. If walls or ceilings show dampness, we find the source and apply the right waterproofing first, so your new paint lasts longer.

Do you install and service ACs?

Yes. We handle AC installation, servicing, repair, gas-related checks, relocation and uninstallation for homes and commercial spaces.

How do I choose the right colour for my home?

Consider lighting, furniture and personal style. Book a consultation and we’ll help you pick shades that fit your space.

Can I get help with colour selection?

Yes. Our colour consultancy is designed to help you choose the perfect shades for your home.
