Signs your AC needs professional attention

Weak airflow, unusual noise, water leakage near the indoor unit, ice on copper lines, or bills that spike without a change in usage are all signals to call for service. Early maintenance prevents compressor damage and keeps indoor air healthier.

Installation done right

  • Accurate room-wise tonnage and inverter recommendations
  • Proper copper thickness, insulation and drainage slope
  • Stable outdoor mounting with vibration isolation
  • Electrical isolation, earthing and stabilizer guidance
  • Commissioning with pressure test and cooling verification

AMC plans that pay for themselves

Annual maintenance visits include filter cleaning, coil care, drain clearing and performance checks. Regular service improves cooling, lowers electricity use, extends equipment life and cuts unexpected breakdowns — especially valuable before peak summer.

General AC FAQs

What AC services does Ghar Upgrade provide?

We provide AC installation, servicing, cleaning, maintenance, troubleshooting, repair, gas-related services, and replacement support for residential and commercial AC systems.

Which types of ACs do you service?

We service commonly used systems including:

  • Split AC
  • Window AC
  • Inverter AC
  • Non-inverter AC
  • Cassette AC
  • Ductable AC
  • Commercial AC systems
How often should an AC be serviced?

For regular household use, AC servicing is generally recommended once or twice a year, depending on usage, environment, and the condition of the unit.

Why is regular AC servicing important?

Regular servicing helps maintain cooling performance, improve airflow, reduce dust buildup, identify potential problems early, and support efficient operation.

Why is my AC not cooling properly?

Poor cooling can be caused by dirty filters or coils, blocked airflow, incorrect settings, refrigerant-related issues, electrical problems, or other mechanical faults. A proper inspection is needed to identify the cause.

Why is my AC making unusual noise?

Unusual noise may result from loose components, fan issues, dirt buildup, vibration, or other mechanical problems. Our technician can inspect the unit and identify the cause.

Why is water leaking from my AC?

Water leakage can occur because of a blocked drain pipe, dirty filters or coils, improper drainage, installation issues, or other faults.

Does AC servicing include cleaning the filters?

Yes. Filter cleaning is an important part of routine AC maintenance. Depending on the service package, additional components such as coils and other accessible parts may also be cleaned.

AC Repair & Maintenance FAQs

How do I know if my AC needs repair?

Common warning signs include:

  • Reduced cooling
  • Water leakage
  • Unusual sounds
  • Bad odour
  • Frequent shutdowns
  • Poor airflow
  • Higher-than-usual electricity consumption
  • AC not switching on
Do you repair inverter ACs?

Yes, technicians can inspect and service inverter AC systems and diagnose issues with the relevant electrical and electronic components.

Do you provide preventive AC maintenance?

Yes. Preventive maintenance can help identify developing problems before they become major repairs.

Can you check AC gas/refrigerant issues?

Yes. If an AC has a suspected refrigerant issue, the system should be inspected to identify the cause before any refrigerant-related work is carried out.

Does low refrigerant always mean the AC needs a refill?

Not necessarily. Low refrigerant can indicate a leak or another system issue. The cause should be identified and addressed rather than treating a refill as the only solution.
