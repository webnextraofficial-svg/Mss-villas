FAQs

General Interior Design FAQs

What interior design services does Ghar Upgrade provide?

We provide complete interior design solutions for homes and selected commercial spaces, including space planning, furniture, modular solutions, false ceilings, lighting, wall finishes, and decor.

Do you provide complete home interior solutions?

Yes. We can help plan and execute interiors for individual rooms as well as complete homes.

Which areas of the home do you design?

We can work on:

  • Living rooms
  • Bedrooms
  • Kitchens
  • Dining areas
  • Pooja rooms
  • Home offices
  • Kids’ rooms
  • Bathrooms
  • Balconies
  • Entryways and other spaces
Do you provide customized interior designs?

Yes. Designs can be customized according to the property’s layout, functional requirements, preferences, and project budget.

Can you work with an existing house?

Yes. We can redesign or upgrade existing spaces without necessarily requiring a complete renovation.

Design & Planning FAQs

Do you provide 2D or 3D interior designs?

Design presentation depends on the project scope. Where applicable, we can provide visual concepts and layouts to help customers understand the proposed design.

Can I choose the colours, materials and finishes?

Yes. Customers can discuss their preferences, while our design team can recommend suitable combinations based on the overall concept.

Can you design interiors according to my budget?

Yes. We can develop the design and material selection around the agreed project budget and priorities.

Do you help with space planning?

Yes. Efficient space planning is an important part of our interior design process. We consider movement, storage, functionality, furniture placement, lighting, and overall usability.

Can you create a modern or traditional interior?

Yes. We can develop different design styles, including modern, contemporary, minimal, traditional, luxury, and functional designs.

Execution FAQs

Do you provide execution along with design?

Yes. Depending on the project, Ghar Upgrade can coordinate design and execution so that the proposed concept is properly implemented.

Do you provide modular kitchen solutions?

Yes. Modular kitchen planning can include layout, storage, cabinets, hardware, finishes, countertops, and other required elements.

Do you provide wardrobes and storage solutions?

Yes. Customized wardrobes and storage solutions can be planned according to available space and storage requirements.

Do you handle false ceiling and lighting work?

Yes. False ceiling, lighting layouts, fixtures, and related electrical coordination can be incorporated into the interior plan.

Do you provide painting and wall-finishing services?

Yes. Wall painting, textures, feature walls, and other suitable finishes can be coordinated as part of the interior project.

Can you coordinate different contractors for one project?

Yes. Depending on the project scope, multiple activities can be coordinated to provide a smoother execution process.

Cost & Project FAQs

How much does interior design cost?

The cost depends on the property size, design requirements, materials, finishes, furniture, level of customization, and scope of work. A detailed quotation can be prepared after understanding the project requirements.

Is there a minimum project size?

This depends on the type of project. We can discuss individual-room projects as well as complete-home requirements.

How long does an interior project take?

The timeline depends on the size and complexity of the project, design approvals, material availability, and execution requirements. A project-specific timeline can be provided after finalizing the scope.

Will I receive a detailed quotation?

Yes. The scope of work, materials, quantities where applicable, and applicable charges can be clearly presented in the quotation.

Can I make changes after the design is finalized?

Design changes can generally be accommodated before execution, subject to the project’s stage and any resulting changes in cost or timeline.

Do you provide after-sales support?

Yes. Ghar Upgrade aims to provide appropriate post-completion support according to the project scope and agreed service terms.
