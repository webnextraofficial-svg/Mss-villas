FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What painting services does Ghar Upgrade provide?

We provide complete painting solutions, including interior painting, exterior painting, texture painting, waterproof coatings, wood and metal painting, and repainting.

How do I choose the right paint for my home?

Our Ghar Upgrade experts help you select the right paint based on the surface, location, durability, finish, budget and the look you prefer.

Which paint brands does Ghar Upgrade use?

We work with leading paint brands based on your requirements and budget. Our team recommends suitable products for each surface.

How long does a painting project take?

The duration depends on the size of the property, surface condition, number of coats and type of paint. We share an estimated timeline before starting the work.

Does Ghar Upgrade inspect the site before giving a quotation?

Yes. We inspect the site to understand the surface condition, measurements, repairs required and painting requirements before preparing the quotation.

Does Ghar Upgrade provide surface preparation?

Yes. Cleaning, crack filling, putty, sanding, primer application and other surface preparation are carried out as required before painting.

Can Ghar Upgrade paint over existing paint?

Yes, if the existing surface is in suitable condition. If there is peeling, dampness, cracks or other damage, we recommend the necessary treatment before repainting.

Does Ghar Upgrade repair cracks before painting?

Yes. We identify and treat suitable surface cracks before painting. For recurring or structural cracks, we may recommend further inspection or treatment.

Can Ghar Upgrade help with colour selection?

Absolutely. Our team can help you choose colour combinations, finishes and shades that suit your home or commercial space.

Does Ghar Upgrade provide waterproofing along with painting?

Yes. Where moisture or leakage is affecting the surface, we provide waterproofing solutions followed by appropriate painting.

Will Ghar Upgrade protect my furniture and flooring during painting?

Yes. We take precautions to protect furniture, flooring, fixtures and other areas from paint splashes and dust.

Does Ghar Upgrade clean the site after the painting work?

Yes. We clean the work area and remove painting-related waste after completion.

How many coats of paint are required?

The number of coats depends on the existing surface, paint type, colour change and required finish. Our team will recommend the right system for your walls.

How can I get a painting quotation from Ghar Upgrade?

Contact Ghar Upgrade and share your location and painting requirements. We will arrange a site inspection and provide a detailed quotation.

Does Ghar Upgrade provide a warranty for painting work?

Warranty terms depend on the type of work, products used and project conditions. Any applicable warranty is clearly mentioned in the quotation.

Does Ghar Upgrade take on both residential and commercial projects?

Yes. We paint homes, apartments, villas, offices, shops and other commercial properties.

Can Ghar Upgrade complete the work within a specific deadline?

We plan the project around your requirements and give you a realistic completion schedule before starting the work.

Does Ghar Upgrade price painting per square foot?

Pricing depends on area, surface condition, paint quality, number of coats, preparation work and other requirements. A site inspection gives the most accurate estimate.
