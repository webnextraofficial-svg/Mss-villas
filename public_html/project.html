About MSS Balaji Grandeur

MSS Balaji Grandeur is a residential project by MSS Builders in Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh. It offers families a choice of 2 BHK homes and duplex homes within a gated community, so you can pick the home that matches your needs and budget while enjoying the same community features.

About MSS Builders

MSS Builders has been building homes in Anantapur for 15 years, with four completed communities that families live in today — including Balaji Villas and MSS Balaji Grandeur. Read more about us.

2 BHK Homes in Anantapur

The 2 BHK homes at MSS Balaji Grandeur are priced at ₹64 Lakhs. They are a practical choice for young families and first-time buyers who want their own home in a secure, well-connected community. Prices are negotiable — call our team to discuss.
